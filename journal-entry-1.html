Journal Entry #1

Date: March 19, 2025

A Miraculous Encounter: The Arrival of Lord Jagannath

Today was my day off, and my day unfolded just as usual, following the routine I have become accustomed to.

But little did I know, a drastic change was about to take place in my life.

A few days ago, a friend of mine had visited Jagannath Puri for a pilgrimage to see Lord Jagannath. During his visit, he had a deeply fulfilling experience, witnessing the divine presence of the Lord.

On his way back, my friend brought prashad (sacred offering) and a small idol of Lord Jagannath as a gift for me, wanting to share his experience and blessings.

When he returned, he tried multiple times to meet me, but unfortunately, we couldn’t connect. The reason was that I had a lot of work in the office, and our schedules never aligned.

In my daily life, there are certain habits that I wish to give up, but somehow, I have been unable to let go of them. Every time I attempt to change, I fail.

Today, I finally got the chance to meet my friend, and I received the prashad and the idol of Lord Jagannath from him. With a heart full of joy, I took the idol back home and shared the moment with my mother.

Throughout the journey, I didn’t open the idol, but when my mother opened it, something unexpected happened. A strange sensation started vibrating through my body, a different kind of energy that felt like something was burning inside me. But I couldn’t feel the source of this sensation.

This feeling made me feel uncertain about why such an energy had suddenly surged within me. It was a moment of confusion.

I shared this experience with another friend of mine, who suggested that it might be a divine miracle from Lord Jagannath.

A few days ago, while having my meal, I was scrolling through YouTube, as I often do. Suddenly, I came across an animated video about Lord Jagannath, and I decided to play it. The video was enlightening, and it spoke about the significance of Lord Jagannath. It resonated deeply with me.

However, after that day, things in my life didn’t seem to go well. I felt as if something was stuck, as if there was unfinished business, and the troubles in my life were increasing.

Back to the present moment: As soon as I saw the idol of Lord Jagannath, a wave of energy surged through my body.

Perhaps, for many days, Lord Jagannath had been trying to give me a sign that He was now coming to my home, and that I needed to bring about changes in my lifestyle. A transformation was necessary.
